The flight plan

Every engagement flies the same profile. The length of each phase depends on your systems; the gates don’t change. Nothing moves to the next phase without a go/no-go.

  1. T−1

    Mission brief

    One working session on the real problem, not a pitch. We look at your data, systems and constraints and agree on what “in production” means for you.

    Gate: written objective + success measure

    Planned

  2. T+0

    Embed

    Access to repos, cloud, data and the people who know them. I sit with your team, join your rituals, and map the system the AI has to live in.

    Gate: dev environment, data access, eval baseline

    Planned

  3. T+1

    First production slice

    A thin end-to-end path running in your infrastructure, on real data, behind your auth, with tracing on. Small, but real, and it proves the route.

    Gate: slice live, observable, evaluated

    Planned

  4. T+n

    Burns

    Short, gated iterations that widen the capability: agents, retrieval, evaluation, model routing, the AI software factory. Each burn ships behind an eval and a regression gate.

    Gate: evals green, cost + latency inside budget

    Planned

  5. EOM

    Handoff & recovery

    Runbooks, dashboards, architecture decisions written down, and your engineers flying it while I’m still in the room. Then I step back.

    Gate: your team operates it without me

    Planned
