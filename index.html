01 — Problems I solve

Your AI demo works. Production doesn't.

Most AI initiatives stall in the gap between a promising prototype and a system people trust. That gap is an engineering problem — and it's the one I work on.

  1. “It works in the notebook.”

    Prototypes that collapse under real data, real users, and real latency budgets. I turn them into observable, testable services with clear failure modes.

  2. “We can't tell if it's getting better.”

    No evals, no baselines, prompt changes shipped on vibes. I build evaluation harnesses, golden datasets, and regression gates so quality is measured, not guessed.

  3. “The agent goes off the rails.”

    Agentic workflows that loop, hallucinate tools, or burn budget. I design bounded agents with explicit contracts, guardrails, human checkpoints, and traces you can debug.

  4. “Our engineers use AI, but delivery didn't speed up.”

    Individual copilots don't change a delivery system. I redesign the system itself — the AI software factory — so AI compounds across the whole SDLC.

  5. “RAG answers are confidently wrong.”

    Retrieval that misses, chunks that mislead, no citations. I fix ingestion, retrieval, ranking, and grounding — and prove it with evals.

  6. “Costs and latency are out of control.”

    Model routing, caching, batching, and right-sizing so you pay for intelligence where it matters and nowhere else.

02 — The AI Software Factory

From ticket to shipped code, on rails.

An AI software factory is an engineered delivery pipeline where coding agents do the heavy lifting and humans stay in control of intent, approval, and judgement. Not a chatbot in your IDE — a system with stages, contracts, and quality gates.

01

Intake

Tickets become AI-ready work packets: intent, acceptance criteria, constraints, verification contract.

02

Context

Repo maps, ADRs, standards, and domain knowledge assembled so agents work from facts, not guesses.

03

Plan

Phased implementation plans with risks and evidence expectations. A human approves before code is written.

04

Build

Coding agents implement in isolated worktrees, test-first, following your repo's own conventions.

05

Verify

Tests, linters, security and quality scans, E2E checks — automated gates every change must pass.

06

Review & Ship

Independent AI review against standards and spec, then a clean PR for human sign-off.

What you get

  • A working factory wired into your Git, CI/CD, and issue tracker
  • Reusable skills, agent guidance and repo harnesses for your codebase
  • Quality gates and audit trails your security team will sign off on
  • Metrics: cycle time, acceptance rate, rework, cost per change
  • Your team trained to operate and extend it

Principles

  • Humans own intent. Agents own toil.
  • Evidence over vibes. Every step leaves an artifact.
  • Bounded autonomy. Clear checkpoints, no runaway agents.
  • Model-agnostic. Swap models without rebuilding the line.
  • Start small. One repo, one workflow, then scale.
