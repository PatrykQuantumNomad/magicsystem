## Product Purpose

Magic System is Patryk Golabek's practice as an **AI Forward Deployed Engineer (AI FDE)**: a human engineer who embeds with a client's team, works directly against their real data, systems and workflows, and ships AI (agents, RAG, evaluation, AI software factories) into production. Success = qualified inbound conversations from teams with real AI problems.

## Positioning

The FDE role applied to AI, delivered by one senior engineer: sits next to the client's data and people, owns outcomes end to end, and leaves behind production systems the client's team can run. Differentiator is depth across both AI engineering and the cloud-native/platform layer AI has to run on (17+ years, former CTO, pre-1.0 Kubernetes adopter).

FDE framing (from https://www.iit.edu/blog/forward-deployed-engineer): an engineer embedded with the customer to design, build and ship custom software, usually AI. "A regular developer focuses on one capability across many customers; a forward deployed engineer focuses on one customer across many capabilities." Contrast to use in copy: a solutions engineer/consultant advises and builds POCs on sample data; an FDE writes production code inside the customer's real infrastructure (RAG, evals, fine-tuning, data integration) and closes the "easy to demo, hard to deploy" gap. Work splits across embedding with the team, building in their stack, and feeding learnings back. Do not quote third-party failure-rate statistics without a citation.

Note: Palantir uses "AI FDE" for an agent that operates Foundry. Here "AI FDE" means the human role; copy must not imply a Palantir affiliation or a Foundry product.

## Operating Context
